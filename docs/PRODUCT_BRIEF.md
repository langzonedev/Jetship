# Product brief

An immediately understandable, replayable flying game for Lang Systems Arcade, inspired by the broad two-touch-control arcade format. Original implementation and graphics; no source or assets copied from the hospital game.

First release: automatic forward flight, hold either screen half to steer, visible pillars, boost rings, satisfying banking/feedback, difficulty progression, local best and immediate retry. Futuristic low-poly visuals, nostalgic sunset and perspective grid. Phone-first controls with desktop keyboard equivalents.

Not included: weapons, multiplayer, accounts, monetisation, telemetry, or arcade-directory publication before operator feedback.
